Square multi-select toggle with optional label.

```jsx
<Checkbox label="I agree to the terms" />
<Checkbox checked={all} indeterminate={some} onChange={toggleAll} label="Select all" />
<Checkbox defaultChecked disabled label="KYC verified" />
```

Checked/indeterminate fill brand blue-900. Controlled or uncontrolled. For single-choice groups use a radio instead.
