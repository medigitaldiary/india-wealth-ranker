Compact status pill — bond ratings, holding states, online/verified indicators, counts.

```jsx
<Badge color="green" dot>Confirmed</Badge>
<Badge color="amber">Pending</Badge>
<Badge color="sky" variant="light">Verified</Badge>
<Badge color="blue" variant="filled" size="sm">AAA</Badge>
<Badge color="gray" variant="stroke">Matured</Badge>
```

Colors map to semantic states (green=success, amber=warning, red=error, blue=information, sky=verified, purple=feature, gray=faded). Variants: `light` (default, tinted), `filled` (solid), `stroke`.
